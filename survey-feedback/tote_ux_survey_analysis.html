Key Headline

Racing is the Tote's undisputed heartland — loyal, frequent, and highly satisfied. However, the Tote is leaving significant revenue on the table in Sports and Games/Casino, where engagement is low, awareness is patchy, and competitive differentiation is unclear. Across all verticals, odds, promotions, and loyalty are the primary reasons customers bet elsewhere.

How Customers Would Improve Racing

🎯

Cross-Card & Multiple Meeting Bets

The single most common improvement request. Customers strongly want to place doubles, trebles, Yankees, Lucky 15s, and accumulators across different race meetings — not just within the same card. This is a core capability gap vs competitors.

💰

More Promotions, Free Bets & Loyalty

The highest volume of feedback. Customers want more frequent free placepots (at least £1, not 50p), "Bet & Get" offers, money back specials, and a visible loyalty programme. Specific nostalgia for the discontinued "Beat Benson" competition and Ten To Follow.

📈

Best Odds Guaranteed (BOG)

BOG is explicitly requested by many customers and is seen as table stakes with competitors. Related requests include: fixed odds option, guaranteed SP match, and ability to take a price with downside protection if SP is lower.

📍

Extra Places on Large Handicap Races

Strong demand for 4 places in fields of 14–16 runners, and 5 places in 17+ runner races. Perceived as a competitive gap vs bookmakers. Specifically applies to each way betting which is popular with this audience.

⚡

App & Website Speed / Usability

Recurring feedback on clunky navigation, Android app slowness, login issues with biometrics, inability to add new bank cards, and the website looking dated. Specific pain point: app glitch that switches placepot to jackpot bet type.

💡

Better Education on Bet Types

Multiple customers don't understand exotic bets (World Pools, Swinger vs Tricast, Exacta). Suggestions include: YouTube "How to Bet" guides, explanation pages on the betting slip itself, and simpler terminology for new or casual bettors.

How Customers Would Improve Sports

🎯

More Free Bets & Promotions

By far the most requested improvement. Customers want regular free bets (not just sign-up offers), bet and get deals, and money back specials. Specifically mentioned: free acca insurance if one leg loses, and bet £10 get £10 rather than bet £10 get £5.

⚽

Super Sub & Player Markets

Multiple customers request "Super Sub" — if a player they bet on is substituted, the bet transfers to the replacement. Also wanted: shots on target, fouls, over/under goals (1.5 goals market), correct score money back, and VAR payout features.

⛳

Better Golf Coverage

Golf is the #1 specific sport requested for improvement — more markets on golf tournaments, better each way terms, group betting, and competitive pricing on majors. Multiple mentions of wanting golf to be a stronger product.

📱

App returns to horse racing page after selecting a football team. Hard to find handicap betting. Saturday morning football should show all domestic matches in a scrollable list rather than highlighted selections only. Pre-built Bet Builder suggestions requested.

Odds in Fractions + Better Prices

Multiple requests for fractional odds display (not just decimals). Competitive odds improvement is the most cited reason to use Bet365 and SkyBet over Tote. Also requested: early prices the night before and Best Odds Guaranteed (BOG).

Revive Unique Tote Products

Several passionate responses about the discontinuation of "Beat Benson" betting competitions and the Ten To Follow competition. Customers feel Tote has become generic — they want unique, skill-based products that differentiate from mainstream bookmakers.

NPS Interpretation

Racing's +24 NPS is the clear standout — loyalty is real and the product works. The Sports NPS of 0 is the most urgent signal in the data: the Tote has as many detractors as promoters on this vertical, meaning word-of-mouth is neutral at best. 06 — Strategic Recommendations

Priority actions by
impact and effort

P1

All Verticals

Improve visibility and communication of the existing loyalty programme

Despite having a loyalty programme, loyalty and reward recognition is still the #1 theme in the qualitative data — customers are not aware of, or not experiencing the benefits of, what the Tote already offers. The priority should be surfacing and communicating the existing programme more clearly within the app, at key moments (post-win, post-festival, on anniversary of account opening), and through direct customer communications.

P2

Sports

Address Bet Builder awareness and value perception

59% of sports bettors haven't tried Tote's Bet Builder. Promote the feature prominently in-app, simplify the journey, and add a competitive incentive (e.g. enhanced odds on first Bet Builder). Adding fractional odds display is a quick win for the existing audience.

P3

Racing

Drive Placepot Turbo awareness and education

41% of Placepot players are unaware of the Turbo. Those who use it are satisfied (68% happy with the 1-hour format), and 44 customers are spending incrementally because of it. A dedicated awareness campaign with clear explainer content could rapidly grow the Turbo pool and revenue.

P4

Sports + Casino

Create cross-sell journeys for Racing-only customers

The existing Racing customer base is loyal and trusted. Personalised nudges (e.g. "You bet on Cheltenham — here's a football offer this weekend") could convert a meaningful portion of the 46% who don't currently use Tote Sports. Games should be similarly surfaced with a first-play incentive.

P5

All Verticals

Improve promotion surfacing and relevance in-app

The Money Back First Goalscorer offer has 84% non-usage — not because it's unpopular but because customers can't find it. Promotions are the second most-cited reason to bet with competitors. A dedicated promotions hub, contextual in-bet prompts, and push notifications would materially improve uptake.

P6

Casino + Live Casino

Expand Casino game range and introduce reload/cashback bonuses

Game selection and promotional offers (free spins, cashback) are the primary reasons Casino customers use Sky Vegas and Bet365 in addition to the Tote. Live Casino players specifically request bonuses, and more poker game formats (e.g. Caribbean Stud). Even a targeted acquisition offer for Live Casino could grow the currently small but satisfied user base.
